For many years, workplace strategy followed a predictable cycle. Organizations designed a workplace, completed a renovation, relocated employees, and considered the project finished until the next major lease event or office redesign. Workplace studies were often conducted only when significant change was already underway, leaving long periods where organizations had little visibility into how their offices were actually performing.
